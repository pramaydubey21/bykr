| **U** | Originality, modification, oral examination (4) | Deliberate departures from the brief, each defensible: (1) **`ride_waypoint` table** (`01_schema.sql` l.153), a downsampled route written at ride end, so `fn_ride_distance_km` and `sp_end_ride` can compute in SQL while raw pings stay in MongoDB. (2) **`authority.base_latitude / base_longitude / radius_km`** (l.214) turn the textual jurisdiction into something `trg_generate_sos` can match spatially (`fn_authorities_for_point`). (3) **`fn_user_can_view_ride` lives in `02_rbac.sql`**, not 03, because a policy binds to its functions when it is created, and 02 runs before 03 (explained in `03_functions.sql` l.5–7). (4) **RETURNING policy fix**, commit `1c9f8a2`: `pol_ride_select` also checks `created_by` on the row itself (`02_rbac.sql` l.148–153), because `INSERT … RETURNING` re-checks the policy and the STABLE function's snapshot cannot see the new row. (5) **Owner connection for the DML demo only** (`withOwnerRolledBack`, `lib/db/index.ts` l.76): 07 writes on behalf of several users and into trigger-only tables, which RLS correctly refuses, so it runs as the owner and always rolls back. Everything else uses `withUser`. (6) Extra integrity beyond the brief: one leader per ride, one open status per participant, no duplicate unordered friendship, `trg_accident_requires_active_ride`, and a deterministic, explainable embedding with no external model. **bykr never contacts an emergency service:** authorities are shown as `MANUAL_ACTION_REQUIRED` (`sp_dispatch_alert`). |
